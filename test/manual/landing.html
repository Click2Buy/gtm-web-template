Landing page

Arrival from a Click2Buy redirection: the "Click2Buy — Page view" tag loads the script, which stores c2bm in the _c2b_attr cookie. To test the consent case, reset the consent and land again: the tag is blocked until you click "Accept all", then fires on cookie_consent_update.

Debug mode of the retailer script (every sale is a validation_test): add c2b_debug=1 to the URL, c2b_debug=0 to turn it off.
